Build logs preserve the engineering process, not just the polished result.

## What Goes Here

- Experiments and prototypes
- Debugging journals
- Deployment notes
- Performance investigations
- Failed approaches
- Retrospectives and lessons learned

## Log Structure

1. Context
2. Goal
3. Attempted approach
4. What broke
5. Fix or next experiment
6. Permanent learning
