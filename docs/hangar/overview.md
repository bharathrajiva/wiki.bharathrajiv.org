and the lessons learned from things that didn't fly quite right the first time.

## Topics

- Frame design, motor/ESC selection, and power budgeting
- Flight controller firmware and tuning
- Telemetry, GPS, and failsafe design
- Sensor payloads and onboard compute
- Build logs: what broke, what got fixed, what's next
